# StatInspector

Your live character attributes on screen — a compact mini-HUD with exactly the stats you
care about, in the order you want.

![Stat inspector](media/stat-inspector.png)

## How to use

1. Install the plugin and launch the game **Modded**.
2. Open StatInspector from the Stellar overlay, or click the gear on the mini-HUD. In
   **All stats**, click a stat to add it to the mini-HUD (click again to remove it). Use the
   search box to find one quickly.

![Stat picker](media/statinspector-settings.png)
3. Open **On the HUD** to see your picks in the order the mini-HUD shows them. Drag a row to
   move it, or click ✕ to remove it.

![Stat order](media/statinspector-order.png)
4. In the **HUD** section, pick how many **Columns** the mini-HUD uses, and raise the
   **Background** slider if the numbers are hard to read over the game.
5. Move the mini-HUD in the HUD layout editor. It shows at its real size there, with sample
   stats if you haven't picked any yet. Its position and shown/hidden state are remembered
   across relaunches.

## Tips

- Values update live, so it's handy for checking how a gear or module swap actually moves
  your numbers.
- **Clear all** asks once more before it removes every stat from the mini-HUD.
- The **?** buttons explain each setting.

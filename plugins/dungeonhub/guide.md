# Dungeon Hub

Every dungeon in one window. Pick a dungeon and a difficulty, then go in Solo, start a Match, or enter with
your Party, without walking to an NPC. Dungeon Hub also replaces the game's full-screen entry confirm with a
small window that shows who is ready.

![Dungeon Hub](media/hub.png)

## Getting started

1. Install the plugin and launch the game **Modded**.
2. Log in to a character and open **Dungeon Hub** in one of these ways:
   - the **Dungeon Hub** button on the right side of the game's Esc menu,
   - the **Dungeon Hub** tile in the Stellar launcher,
   - a hotkey of your own (see *Hotkeys* below).
3. Pick a tab, pick a dungeon on the left, then choose a difficulty and **How to go** on the right.

The header shows whether you're solo or in a party (and whether you're the leader). The window opens on the
tab you used last.

## Choosing a dungeon

The hub has five tabs: **Dungeons**, **Raid**, **World Boss**, **Hunt** and **Other**.

**Dungeons**

- **OPEN TODAY** at the top lists today's Unstable dungeons, marked **UNSTABLE**. The regular dungeons follow
  under **DUNGEONS**. When no Unstable dungeon is open, the list has no headings.
- Under **DIFFICULTY**, pick **Normal**, **Hard** or **Master**. Choosing **Master** shows its 20 levels:
  - green = cleared, plain = the next level to clear, dim = locked;
  - a coloured underline marks the levels that allow both **Solo** and **Match**.

**Raid**

- Every raid the game offers right now, with **Easy**, **Hard** and **Nightmare** where the raid has them.

**What the tiles tell you**

- **Recommended**: the score the game recommends for this difficulty.
- **Your score**: green when you meet the recommendation, amber when you're below it.
- **First clear**: whether the first-clear reward is still waiting (**Reward ready**) or already **Claimed**.
- **Rewards left**: how many rewarded runs you have left. At 0 you can still enter; you just won't get the
  rewards.

## How to go

Three buttons at the bottom of the panel:

- **Solo**: enter on your own, with bots.
- **Match**: matchmaking. While it runs, the bar at the bottom shows **Matching** with the time spent and a
  **Cancel** button.
- **Party**: enter with your party.

A button is greyed out when it can't be used right now, and the line under it says why, for example
"not in a party" or "needs 3+ in the party (you have 1) - use Match". In a party, only the leader can start
an entry or a match.

## World Boss, Hunt and Other

- **World Boss**: this week's boss, your rewards left, whether the event is open and when it opens next.
  World Boss is **Match** only.
- **Hunt**: the weekly hunt and **Guild Hunt**. Guild Hunt can be entered from here (Normal or Hard,
  **Solo** or **Party**) while it is open and you're in a guild.
- **Other**: **Bureau Commissions** comes first. Accept the daily quest, see today's events and how many are
  done, and **Enter** an event directly. **Go to the board** takes you to the Pioneer Bureau board.
- The remaining activities have an **Open** button that takes you to the game's own page for them.

## Dungeon Confirm

When a party starts an entry or a match is found, the game normally covers the whole screen to ask everyone
to confirm. Dungeon Hub shows a small window instead. It works for 5-player dungeons, raids and match found.

![Dungeon Confirm](media/confirm.png)

- The dungeon, its difficulty and a countdown at the top.
- **AFFIXES**: the dungeon's affixes, when it has any.
- **PARTY** (or **RAID**, split into four groups): every player with their class, score and state:
  **Ready**, **Waiting…** or **Declined**. Your own row is outlined.
- **Confirm** and **Decline** at the bottom. For a match found, Decline asks once more (**Decline?**) so you
  can't press it by accident. If you started the entry as leader, you get **Cancel Entry** instead.
- When everyone is ready, the window closes and you go in. If someone declines or time runs out, the window
  says who, then closes on its own.
- The window stays on top of your other Stellar windows, so it is never hidden behind them.

## Settings

Click the gear button (**⚙**) in the Dungeon Hub header to open **Dungeon Hub Settings**.

![Dungeon Hub Settings](media/settings.png)

**CONFIRM SCREEN**

- **Replace the game's confirm screen**: turn the small window on or off. It is on by default. When it's off,
  you get the game's own full-screen confirm.
- **Preview 5-player** / **Preview raid**: see the window with example players, without a party. The buttons
  only close the preview.

**SOUNDS** (these play only on the small confirm window)

- **Sound when it opens**: a sound when the confirm window appears.
- **Countdown ticks**: a tick each second for the last 10 seconds while you still have to answer.
- **Volume**: for both sounds. Press **▶** next to a sound to hear it, and **■** to stop it.
- **Open sounds folder**: put your own .wav or .mp3 files there, then pick them in the sound lists.
  **Default** goes back to the built-in sound. If a file can't be played, the default sound plays and the
  settings tell you which file it was.
- You hear these sounds even when the game is muted or in the background.

**WINDOW**

- **Custom window opacity**: off = the Dungeon Hub windows follow your theme's Window Opacity. On = set your
  own **Opacity** for all of them.

## Hotkeys

Both start unbound. Assign a key in **Stellar Settings → Hotkeys**:

- **Toggle Dungeon Hub window**: open or close the hub.
- **Dungeon: Toggle Confirm Replacement**: turn the small confirm window on or off.

## Notes

- Dungeon Hub opens only while you're in the world, and its windows close when you log out.
- If the small window can't show an entry request, the game's own confirm screen appears as usual.

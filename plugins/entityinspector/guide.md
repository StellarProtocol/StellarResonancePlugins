# EntityInspector

Inspect any player — stats, gear, skills, outfit and dyes — in one window you can open on anyone,
including yourself.

![The Overview tab](media/entityinspector-overview.png)

## How to open it

1. Install the plugin and launch the game **Modded**.
2. Open an inspection from either place:
   - the **Inspect** button on a player's profile card, or
   - the **Inspect** item on a CombatMeter row (with CombatMeter installed).

## The header

The top of the window shows the player's name, class and spec, level, Ability Score and
Illusion-Breaking. A status tells you how fresh the information is:

- **Nearby · live** — the player is near you, so everything updates live.
- **Far · profile from N min ago** — the player is away from you; what you see comes from their profile.
- **Session view** — you're looking at a saved session. Close it to go back to live.

On the left, the **portrait** shows the player in 3D (**drag** to turn, **mouse wheel** to zoom), with a
**Weapon: On / Off** switch, their HP bar (with shield) and their imagines.

## The tabs

- **Overview** — stats grouped into panels in two columns, plus guild, party, equipped title and
  fashion / mount / weapon skin collection points.
- **Gear** — the weapon on its own row on top, the other pieces in a grid. **Click an item** to see its
  details. Your own gear shows its upgraded gear score, refine and upgrade stage.
- **Skills** — Battle Imagines and skills.
- **Wardrobe** — every outfit piece with its dye slots, numbered like the game's dye screen
  (**Default** means undyed). **Click a colour** to copy its colour code.
- **Location** — where the player is on the map. **Drag** to pan, **mouse wheel** to zoom, **Full map**
  and **Focus** to move around.
- **Party** — the player's party. **Click a member** to inspect them.

The round **?** at the top right shows a short help text.

## What the game doesn't share

- Other players only share some of their stats, so only those are shown, and sections with nothing to
  show are hidden.
- HP and imagines show while the player is near you. Skills and gear appear once you've been near them.
- The game doesn't share other players' gear upgrade stage. Upgradeable pieces show a **+** after the
  gear score (for example **GS 260+**), meaning it may be higher.

## Notes

- Requires Stellar 2.32.0 or newer.
- The plugin is available in English, 日本語, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino and 한국어.

# Wardrobe

Save the outfits you wear and switch between them instantly — with one click or a hotkey — with a live 3D
preview of each outfit.

![The Wardrobe window](media/wardrobe-window.png)

## How to use

1. Install the plugin and launch the game **Modded**.
2. Dress up in-game however you like, then open **Wardrobe** from the Stellar launcher (or bind the
   **Open Wardrobe** hotkey) and click **Save current outfit** at the top right. The outfit is added to the
   list for that character.
3. Change your outfit in-game, then bring a saved one back:
   - Click **Wear** on its row, or
   - Press its hotkey — the first 8 outfits in the list map to **Apply Outfit 1**–**Apply Outfit 8**
     (bind them in **Stellar Settings › Hotkeys**; they ship unbound).

The round **?** next to **Save current outfit** shows a short help text. The line at the top tells you which
saved outfit you're wearing and how many outfits you have.

## The outfit list

Each row shows, from left to right:

- **Drag handle** — drag the row up or down to change its place. The first 8 rows are the hotkey slots.
- **Hotkey badge** — the key you bound for that slot (for example **F1**). If the slot has no key yet it
  shows **H1**–**H8**; rows after the 8th just show their number.
- **Name** and **piece count** (for example "12 pcs").
- **Wear** — put this outfit on. The outfit you're wearing right now shows **Worn** instead; click **Worn** to
  put it on again (for example after changing dyes).
- **Pencil** — rename the outfit. Press **Enter** or **✓** to save the name, **✕** to cancel.
- **Refresh** — replace this outfit with what you're wearing now. It keeps its name and place, and asks
  you to confirm with **Replace** first.
- **✕** — delete the outfit. It asks you to confirm with **Delete** first.

While a rename or a confirm is open, the other rows wait until you finish it.

## Preview

Hover any row to see that outfit on a live 3D model of your character. **Drag** to turn it, **Shift+drag**
to move it, and use the **mouse wheel** to zoom.

The preview shows the outfit exactly as saved, including the pieces it hides. The **Weapon: On / Off** chip
shows or hides your weapon on the model; when the outfit has a saved weapon skin, the weapon is shown in that
skin.

## Shown and hidden pieces

Each outfit remembers which pieces you hid (like a hat) and hides them again when you wear it. Outfits saved
before version 1.5.0 don't have this yet: wear the outfit with the pieces shown or hidden the way you want,
then press **Refresh** → **Replace** on its row once.

## Notes

- Outfits are saved **per character**.
- Switching runs through the game's own fashion system, which applies its own rules (for example, you
  can't change outfit in combat) — the game shows a banner if a switch isn't allowed right now.
- The plugin is available in English, 日本語, ไทย, Bahasa Indonesia, Filipino and 한국어.
